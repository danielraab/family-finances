## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A caller can preview upcoming occurrences of their visible recurring transactions, bounded by a required cutoff

`GET /api/recurring-transactions/preview` SHALL accept the same
`account_id` filter (repeatable, defaulting to every account the caller
has any permission on) `GET /api/recurring-transactions` accepts, plus
`category_id`, `category_mode` (`exact`|`subtree`, mirroring
`account-entries`'s `GET /api/entries`), `tag_id`, and a required `to`
(an RFC3339 timestamp). It SHALL respond `{ items: [...] }`, one item per
projected future occurrence of each matching, non-deleted recurring
transaction whose parent account is not soft-deleted, ordered by
`booking_timestamp` ascending. Each item SHALL carry the same content
fields a materialized entry from that template would (`account_id`,
`account_currency`, `title`, `description`, `category_id`,
`counterparty`, `location`, `tag_ids`, `amount`), plus
`recurring_transaction_id`, `booking_timestamp` (the projected date), and
`overdue` (`true` when `booking_timestamp` is before the current date in
the caller's resolved timezone). No item SHALL carry an `id` — a
projected occurrence is never persisted. A request with no `to` SHALL be
rejected (`400`); there is no default cutoff.

#### Scenario: Basic projection

- **WHEN** a monthly recurring transaction with `next_suggested_date:
  2026-10-01` and no linked entries is previewed with
  `to: 2027-01-01T00:00:00Z`
- **THEN** the response includes four occurrences of that template, dated
  2026-10-01, 2026-11-01, 2026-12-01, and 2027-01-01, none marked
  `overdue`

#### Scenario: Preview requires a cutoff

- **WHEN** `GET /api/recurring-transactions/preview` is called with no
  `to`
- **THEN** the request is rejected (`400`)

#### Scenario: Filtering narrows the previewed templates

- **WHEN** `GET /api/recurring-transactions/preview` is called with
  `category_id={id}` and `to` set
- **THEN** only occurrences of recurring transactions matching that
  category (per `category_mode`) are included

#### Scenario: No accessible templates returns an empty list

- **WHEN** a caller with no recurring transactions on any visible account
  calls the preview endpoint with a valid `to`
- **THEN** the response is `200` with an empty `items` list

### Requirement: An overdue recurring transaction contributes exactly one preview row, not one per missed interval

A recurring transaction whose `next_suggested_date` is before the current
date (in the caller's resolved timezone) SHALL still contribute that date
as a single preview item, marked `overdue: true`. Subsequent items for
that template SHALL be generated by repeatedly advancing one interval at
a time, but only dates that are on or after the current date, or that
overdue anchor date itself, SHALL be emitted — an intermediate date that
is still before the current date SHALL be skipped (advanced past, not
emitted) rather than added as an additional overdue row.

#### Scenario: A single overdue anchor row, not one per missed day

- **WHEN** a daily recurring transaction's `next_suggested_date` is 10
  days in the past and the preview is generated with a `to` that reaches
  30 days into the future
- **THEN** the response includes exactly one item dated 10 days ago
  (`overdue: true`), followed by items for today and each subsequent day
  through the cutoff — not one item per day between the anchor and today

#### Scenario: A non-overdue template has no overdue row

- **WHEN** a recurring transaction's `next_suggested_date` is today or
  later
- **THEN** none of its preview items are marked `overdue`

### Requirement: Preview generation is bounded independently of the caller-supplied cutoff

Each recurring transaction's contribution to a preview response SHALL be
further bounded by that template's own `ends_on`, when set — no item
SHALL be generated with a `booking_timestamp` after `ends_on`, even when
the caller's `to` reaches further. Independently, generation for a single
recurring transaction SHALL stop after 366 items regardless of `to` or
`ends_on`, a defensive bound against a short interval combined with a far
cutoff.

#### Scenario: ends_on clamps the preview tighter than the requested cutoff

- **WHEN** a recurring transaction has `ends_on` one month from now and
  the preview is requested with a `to` six months out
- **THEN** no item for that template is dated after its `ends_on`

#### Scenario: A template already past its end date contributes nothing

- **WHEN** a recurring transaction's `ends_on` is in the past
- **THEN** it contributes no items to the preview response, regardless of
  its `next_suggested_date`

### Requirement: Previewing never creates, links, or otherwise mutates any entry or recurring transaction

`GET /api/recurring-transactions/preview` SHALL be a read-only operation:
calling it, at any frequency, with any parameters, SHALL NOT create an
entry, link an entry, or change any recurring transaction. The manual
"Create transaction" flow remains the only way a projected occurrence
becomes a real entry.

#### Scenario: Repeated previewing has no side effects

- **WHEN** `GET /api/recurring-transactions/preview` is called multiple
  times in a row with the same parameters
- **THEN** each response is identical and no entry or recurring
  transaction is created, modified, or linked as a result
